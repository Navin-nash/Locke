# Locke — Brand Identity & Logo Explorations

**Project:** Locke (neatHack 48h Hackathon)  
**Date:** October 2026  
**Role:** Brand & Visual Identity Lead  
**Taglines:** *"Know who's really hiring."* / *"Evidence before trust."*  

---

## 1. Brand Context & Strategic Foundations

### The Problem & Audience
Recruitment fraud has moved past obvious misspelled phishing emails. Students, fresh graduates, remote job seekers, and career changers are targeted with sophisticated impersonations: legitimate company names, real recruiter personas, and plausible job descriptions, but counterfeit recruiter email domains, lookalike interview URLs, fee scams, and malicious redirects.

When candidates feel uncertain, existing tools fail in two ways:
1. **Generic AI "Risk Scanners":** Produce arbitrary, unexplained scores like *"92% Safe"*, creating false confidence.
2. **Aggressive Blockers / Alarmist Scanners:** Flash red sirens and label anything non-standard as "FRAUD", terrifying anxious job seekers and causing them to forfeit legitimate opportunities.

### The Locke Philosophy
Named after **John Locke**—father of British empiricism (*knowledge originates from verifiable evidence and experience*)—with a natural phonetic and conceptual duality to a **Lock** (*safety, containment, protection*).

Locke operates on three immutable tenets:
1. **Evidence Before Trust:** Never accept claims at face value, but never issue false accusations.
2. **Forensic Calm:** A quiet, methodical, clear-eyed investigator rather than a frantic antivirus scanner.
3. **The Safe Route:** Do not merely diagnose risk; illuminate the authentic, independently verified path to apply.

### Aesthetic Persona & Bans
- **Aesthetic Persona:** Calm, forensic, architectural, trustworthy, warm-institutional.
- **Categorical Bans:**
  - ❌ **No AI Purple / Violet gradients:** Strictly forbidden. No cyber-futuristic purple glows.
  - ❌ **No Neon Hacker Cyberpunk:** No acid matrix green, neon cyan circuit traces, or skull/shield tropes.
  - ❌ **No Cartoon Padlock Cliché:** No round yellow tumblers, childish keyholes, or stock clipart locks.

### Concept A: The Empirical Monogram ("The Caliper Lock") — *Selected Direction*

#### Metaphor & Semantics
A bespoke, architectural letterform **'L'** that integrates the precise mechanical geometry of an open/closed forensic caliper and an architectural lock shackle. 

The vertical stem represents the empirical baseline—grounded, unyielding, authoritative. The horizontal base extends into a precision vernier shelf with a subtle calibrated notch or coordinate indicator. Suspended above is a crisp, arched shackle loop that locks perfectly into the vertical stem's optical rhythm.

#### Visual Form & Geometry
- **Grid:** Built on an exact 256×256 unit coordinate grid using 45° chamfers and strict 16-unit stroke baselines.
- **Form:** An asymmetric, interlocking harmony where a disciplined shackle arc descends into a bold, solid foundation block.
- **Negative Space:** The inner counter forms an architectural doorway or keyway—a symbol of access granted through empirical proof.

#### Strengths & Small-Scale Resilience
- **Scalability:** Unrivaled structural clarity down to 16×16px favicon rendering. The primary silhouette has strong mass distribution.
- **Tone:** Balances classic collegiate/institutional weight (John Locke's academic pedigree) with precision modern product engineering.
- **Single-Color Test:** Holds 100% fidelity in solid black, solid white on dark slate, or embossed foil.

#### Risks & Mitigation
- *Risk:* Could look too rigid if rendered purely in monoline wireframe.
- *Mitigation:* Balanced with warm, tactile editorial tones (Deep Indigo `#162031` / Slate Teal `#0D5C63` and Warm Cream `#F7F5F0` instead of sterile pitch black).

### Concept B: The Forensic Prism ("The Spectrum Separator")

#### Metaphor & Semantics
Directly visualizes the investigator agent team's core task: taking a noisy, multi-channel claim (recruiter message, screenshot, link) and passing it through an analytical aperture that refracts it into four discrete, independent evidence strands (*Organization*, *Vacancy*, *Recruiter*, *Channel*).

#### Visual Form & Geometry
- An equilateral triangular or hexagonal aperture with subtle faceted planes.
- Incoming ambiguous ray at the left converges at a precision focal pinhole; three distinct geometric vectors emerge rightward in parallel, aligned order.

#### Critique & Why Rejected
- While conceptually rich for an internal agent architecture diagram, at small scales (16–24px) the multi-facet lines blend together or become illegible noise.
- Carries too much visual association with optics physics tools, scientific computing libraries, or data analytics platforms, losing the immediate reassurance of safety and personal agency required by an anxious student.

---

### Concept C: The Verified Portal ("The Safe Route")

#### Metaphor & Semantics
Focuses on the output deliverable: the **Safe Route**. A padlock shackle whose bottom does not close into a dead end, but instead opens forward into a clean perspective corridor or forward-pointing chevron path.

#### Visual Form & Geometry
- Continuous outer loop forming a rounded shield or arch; internal negative space forms an upward directional beacon / road.

#### Critique & Why Rejected
- Padlock arches with roads or arrows inside are easily confused with navigation apps, transport logistics, or generic security badges.
- Tends to drift close to the security-cliché boundary that Locke explicitly wants to avoid.

---

## 3. Justification of the Selected Direction: Concept A ("The Empirical Monogram")

We have unanimously selected **Concept A: The Empirical Monogram** as Locke's brand identity.

1. **Direct Alignment with the Name & Purpose:**
   It unites **John Locke** (the letter 'L', academic precision, editorial truth) and the **Lock** (containment, security, safe passage) without cheap literalism.
2. **Forensic Distinction in Market:**
   While every AI product uses sparkling asterisks (`✦`) or neon gradients, and every security tool uses cartoon shields or green checkmark locks, Locke stands apart with quiet, bespoke typographical authority. It looks like a high-end investigative institution or an exacting investigative journalist desk.
3. **Exceptional Small-Size & Icon Utility:**
   The bold vertical stem, calibrated corner junction, and geometric arch retain 100% legibility in a 16px browser favicon, a 32px tab icon, mobile app home screen tiles, and GitHub avatars.
4. **Motion Potential:**
   The arch shackle and stem provide an instinctive two-part motion narrative: the shackle scans/descends like an analytical caliper, seating crisply with a damped magnetic snap when evidence is verified.
